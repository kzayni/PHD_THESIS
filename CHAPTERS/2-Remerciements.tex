A PhD is an incredible journey, filled with ups and downs, challenges and achievements, and a true rollercoaster of emotions. Although it is, in many ways, an individual endeavor, reaching the end is far from a solitary accomplishment. It is the result of a collective effort and the invaluable support of supervisors, family, friends, and colleagues who accompany us along the way.

First and foremost, I would like to express my deepest gratitude to my supervisor, Prof. Éric Laurendeau, for giving me this wonderful opportunity. From day one, his support has been extraordinary. He has always pushed us to become the best versions of ourselves while constantly providing us with incredible opportunities to learn and grow. I am especially grateful for the many discussions we shared, not only about research but also about the broader aerospace industry, which continuously deepened my understanding and perspective. Beyond research, I will also fondly remember the many stories and conversations about hockey, tennis, and everything in between that made this journey all the more enjoyable.

To my laboratory colleagues, thank you for the countless discussions, exchanges, and moments we shared throughout the years. You made the laboratory not only a stimulating place to conduct research, but also a genuinely enjoyable place to work every day.

I would also like to thank my friends, for their support, encouragement, and friendship throughout this journey. Thank you for all the precious moments we shared and for always being there, despite the long distances and time zones that separated us.

To my family, Baba (Samir), Mama (Fatmé), Rachad, Nazem, Layla, and Rola, thank you for your endless love, care, and unwavering support throughout this journey. I owe this achievement, and every success I accomplish, in large part to you. Your sacrifices, guidance, and unconditional support have shaped the person I am today. I would also like to sincerely thank Mr. Samir and Mme. Mona Meskaoui for their kindness, encouragement, and continuous support throughout my academic journey.

Finally, to my lovely wife, Leila, who patiently listened to my endless ramblings and rants about research, simulations, and code. Your love, patience, care, and support carried me through the most difficult moments and made the happiest ones even more meaningful. I could not have reached this point without you.

None of this would have been possible without all of you. 

Sincerely, thank you \ldots
